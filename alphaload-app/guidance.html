🗓️ Beginner Workout Plans

Foundation Plan

  • 3 days per week
  • 2 sets of 10–12 reps on each machine
  • Focus on steady form and breathing

Strength Starter

  • 4 days per week
  • Use lighter weights for the first 2 weeks
  • Add 2.5–5kg only when form feels solid

Recovery + Cardio

  • 1–2 easy cardio sessions
  • Mobility work after training
  • Rest days stay just as important as gym days
